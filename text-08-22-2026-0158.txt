Gravity falls hackpad

A custom Gravity Falls hemed hackpad that can encode and decode ciphers from the series.

Features:

- Encode and decode Gravity Falls ciphers
- OLED display
- Rotary encoder
- Four switches
- Softwaare designed with Python
- Custom-designed PCB

How I built it

The hackpad uses a Seeed XIAO RP2040, OLED screen, rotary encoder, and four switches.

I designed the PCB myself using KiCad and wrote the software in Python. One of the biggest challenges was learning KiCad and finding the correct footprints for each component.

Status of project: ready to order components
